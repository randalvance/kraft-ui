Add twelve small components for feedback and navigation. `Banner` is a full-width paper strip that pairs a `control` with a line of consequence, and `Callout` is a bordered note with an optional `action`, including a danger form. `EmptyState` is a dashed placeholder with a note and an optional action. `Toast` is a kraft slip with Undo, a close button, and an optional `duration` that waits while hovered or focused; `ToastRegion` is the landmark that stacks toasts. `Stat` prints a large handwritten figure over a caption in five tones. `Timeline` draws ruled history rows or a horizontal schedule lane, and `Stepper` draws a vertical pipeline or a compact step track with `done`, `current`, `upcoming`, and `blocked` steps. `TagInput` is a field of removable slips with an inline add field for use in `FormField`. `Pager` has previous, next, a range, and page dots; `Breadcrumb` marks the current page; `Toolbar` and `ToolbarGroup` give grouped actions one roving tab stop with start and end groups; and `CollapsibleGroup` is a native disclosure with a tinted header, a count, and one-open-at-a-time groups through a shared `name`. Adds the `BannerProps`, `CalloutProps`, `EmptyStateProps`, `ToastProps`, `ToastDismissReason`, `ToastRegionProps`, `ToastPlacement`, `StatProps`, `StatSize`, `TimelineProps`, `TimelineItem`, `TimelineOrientation`, `StepperProps`, `StepperStep`, `StepperVariant`, `StepStatus`, `TagInputProps`, `PagerProps`, `BreadcrumbProps`, `BreadcrumbItem`, `ToolbarProps`, `ToolbarGroupProps`, and `CollapsibleGroupProps` types, and the `--kraft-content-sage` ink role for green text that stays readable on paper, kraft, and cardboard.
