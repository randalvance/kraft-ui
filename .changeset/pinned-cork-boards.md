Add CorkBoard, a cork board in a wooden frame with a scrolling body and grid, free-position, and column layouts. CorkBoardItem places things by coordinates with a z-order and can be moved with the arrow keys. CorkBoardColumn adds a pinned kraft header with a name and count for Kanban boards. Pin draws a tack in four tones as a decoration or as a button to unpin.
