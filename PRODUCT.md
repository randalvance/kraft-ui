# Kraft UI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React 18 and 19 with TypeScript. An installable npm package with an explicitly imported stylesheet and CSS custom properties. No Tailwind or CSS-in-JS requirement.

## Users

React application developers building interfaces and exploring reusable components in Storybook.

## Product Purpose

Provide reusable primitives inspired by paper, cardboard, and stickers. Developers can compose interfaces with a consistent material vocabulary and predictable native HTML behavior.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The library includes tokens, scoped Paper and Night themes, Box, Stack, Inline, Text, Heading, Surface, Mat, Tape, PaperPanel, Card, CardAddTile, StickyNote, CorkBoard, CorkBoardItem, CorkBoardColumn, Pin, Avatar, AvatarGroup, AvatarPicker, Button, IconButton, Pinwheel, Icon, Link, Separator, Badge, Sticker, StatusDot, StatusSticker, Stamp, Tag, Banner, Callout, EmptyState, Toast, ToastRegion, Stat, Timeline, Stepper, Meter, SlotMeter, Gauge, Sparkline, TagInput, Pager, Breadcrumb, Toolbar, ToolbarGroup, CollapsibleGroup, Checkbox, CheckboxGroup, CheckboxStatus, Toggle, FormField, TextField, Textarea, SearchField, Kbd, Composer, InlineEdit, RadioGroup, SegmentedControl, ToggleChips, Select, ActionableList, ActionableListItem, TreeView, Table, TableRowAction, DescriptionList, Prose, CodeBlock, Code, Blockquote, Tabs, Tooltip, TooltipProvider, Popover, Menu, Dialog, Drawer, ResizablePane, AppShell, and RailTile. Components support native props and refs where applicable, server rendering, and current evergreen browsers. Applications control font loading. Texture, motion, and decoration tilt are configurable. Sticker marks remain upright, and glyphs on badges and stickers print in the mark's ink; Avatar rotation is opt-in. The `--kraft-content-danger` ink role gives applications a danger text color that stays readable on every material in both themes; `--kraft-content-sage` does the same for a green ink; `--kraft-red-2` and `--kraft-blue-2` stay readable as text on Night paper, and `--kraft-blue-deep` carries the deep blue for edges and faces. `Tone` names the five shared tones (neutral, blue, sage, danger, attention) and `ExtendedTone` adds six palette tones (ochre, teal, violet, plum, blush, navy) for pieces that carry their own colour, such as a workspace, a rail tile, or a model chip. Each palette tone has a face, a cut edge, and an on-colour ink in both themes (`--kraft-teal-face`, `--kraft-teal-edge`, `--kraft-on-teal`, and likewise for the others), and its label keeps 4.5:1 on every material. Checkbox and toggle faces switch with the theme at once rather than fading. Round stickers, avatars, and small icon buttons use equal width and height.

`Avatar` and `AvatarProps` draw an image (`src`), monogram (`initial`), or icon (`glyph`) as a white-bordered portrait. `size` sets the diameter in pixels (default 36, including 18–84px portraits); `tone` uses the five shared tones. `alt` is required unless `decorative` is true. `tilt` sets optional theme-controlled rotation. `status` takes an `AvatarStatus` with a required `label` and a `working`, `waiting`, `blocked`, `done`, or `idle` tone; the label joins the accessible name and the corner marks differ in shape or stroke as well as color. The native span and ref represent one named image. `AvatarGroup` and `AvatarGroupProps` take `label`, `avatars`, shared `size`, and `max` (default 4), overlap a native list of portraits, and name the remaining count. Its native props and ref reach the list. `AvatarPicker` and `AvatarPickerProps` take `label`, `name`, `options`, `size`, `value` or `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `description`, `hideLabel`, `required`, and `disabled`. Each `AvatarOption` supplies a unique `value`, accessible `label`, portrait content, and optional tone, tilt, status, description, and disabled state. Native fieldset props and the ref reach the fieldset. Native radios support form submission, reset, and keyboard navigation; selection has a portrait ring and focus a separate outline around a target of at least 44px.

`Text` and `TextProps` set copy with `variant` (`TextVariant`: `body`, `label`, `label-sm`, `hand`, `serif`, `lead`, `code`) and `tone` (`TextTone`: `default`, `muted`, `faint`, `danger`, `attention`). The hand variant takes `size` (`HandSize`: `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`, `display`) for 17 to 52px handwriting, and `lead` is an italic serif line. Every tone keeps 4.5:1 contrast on the desk, paper, kraft, and cardboard in both themes. `measure={false}` removes the 72ch cap, which never applies to code text in a `pre`. `Heading` and `HeadingProps` take `size` (`HeadingSize`: serif `sm`, `md`, `lg`; handwritten `title-sm`, `title`, `display`) and `balance={false}` to turn off balanced wrapping. `Link` and `LinkProps` add `tone="inherit"` and `underline="hover"` so a link inside a heading takes the heading ink and shows its underline on hover and keyboard focus. All three forward native props and refs; Text and Heading choose their element with `as`.

`Mat` and `MatProps` offer a paper inset with `padding` inside a frame with `tone="kraft" | "amber" | "sage" | "red"`. Choose the frame element with `as`. `Tape` and `TapeProps` position a masking-tape strip at any edge or corner with `placement`. `Pinwheel` and `PinwheelProps` provide the shared busy indicator at `size="row" | "button"`. It stays still when theme motion is off or the system prefers reduced motion. Tape and Pinwheel accept an accessible `label` or native ARIA labelling attributes and are decorative by default. `Button` and `IconButton` use the decorative Pinwheel while `loading` and expose their own busy state.

`Button` and `ButtonProps` accept a decorative `leading` icon and use `wrap` to opt into multiline labels. `ButtonVariant` covers the eleven raised tones of `ExtendedTone`, `ghost` with a flat outline, `text` with optional `tone="danger"`, and `dashed` for adding items. `IconButton` and `IconButtonProps` require `aria-label` and take a decorative `badge` slot for a count sticker at the top-end corner. `IconButtonVariant` covers the same eleven raised tones and the faceless `bare` style for dense rows. Both forward native button props and refs, support `size="sm" | "md" | "lg"`, and default to `type="button"`. Application-managed `aria-pressed` flattens the face while preserving the control's alignment in wrapping rows. The `loading` state swaps the leading icon or action glyph for Pinwheel, keeps the label and badge, and disables activation.

`Card` and `CardProps` provide a paper article with `header`, `media`, `children` (body), `footer`, and `corner`. `tone` adds a spine in any `CardTone` (the same set as `ExtendedTone`), including ochre, teal, violet, plum, blush, and navy. `off` dims the paper while keeping actions available. `selected` marks the edge and adds a check. `tilt` sets optional theme-controlled rotation. `as="a"` requires `href` and `as="button"` supplies native activation. Both require `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label`, expose selection via `aria-current` or `aria-pressed`, and leave slot controls independently operable. A custom interactive region can set `data-card-interactive` for its own pointer hit area. Native props and the ref reach the action or static article. `className` and `style` style the outer article. `CardAddTile` and `CardAddTileProps` provide a dashed grid button with a plus, a required `label`, native button props, and a button ref. `--kraft-card-min-height` sets the base height of both (default `10rem`), and `size="compact"` (`CardSize`) drops it on either, so the piece is as tall as its content.

`StickyNote` and `StickyNoteProps` provide a curled paper note with `header`, `children`, and `footer` slots and a numeric `tilt` in degrees that follows the theme setting. The note adds no heading; one placed in `header` takes the level that follows the page outline. `StickyNoteTone` covers `attention`, `blush`, `blue`, `sage`, and `ochre`. `StickyNoteStatus` covers `waiting` (hatched) and `done` (struck body), with visible status text customizable via `statusLabel`. Choose `as="article"` for rich content or `as="button"` for native whole-note activation with optional controlled `selected` state. Native props and the ref reach the chosen element.

`CorkBoard` and `CorkBoardProps` draw cork in a wooden frame with a scrolling body sized by `height` or `maxHeight` and named by `scrollLabel` when it overflows. `CorkBoardLayout` covers `grid` (cells at least `minItemWidth` wide), `free`, and `columns`. `CorkBoardItem` and `CorkBoardItemProps` hold one pinned thing with an optional `pin`; on a free board `x`, `y`, and `z` place and stack it. With `label` and `onMove` it is a named tab stop that the arrow keys move by `step` pixels, reporting a `CorkBoardPosition`. `CorkBoardColumn` and `CorkBoardColumnProps` provide a Kanban column: a named section with a pinned kraft header showing `name`, `count`, and an optional `countLabel`, with `pinTone` and a `headingLevel` that defaults to 2, the level under a page `h1`. `Pin` and `PinProps` draw a tack with a highlight in a `PinTone` of `red`, `blue`, `green`, or `amber`. It is decorative by default, a named image with `label`, or a native button with `as="button"` and a required `label`.

`AppShell` and `AppShellProps` lay out the diorama page: a `rail` in a navigation landmark named by `railLabel` with an optional `railFooter`, an optional `topBar` header, an optional cardboard `board` in a complementary landmark named by the required `boardLabel`, and one paper sheet in `main` with an optional fixed `sheetHead` over a body that scrolls on its own and is named by `sheetLabel` when it overflows. The shell fills the viewport with a 3px margin, and each region scrolls independently. When the shell is 720px wide or less, the board becomes a dismissible overlay: a rail toggle with `aria-expanded` opens it and moves focus inside, and a close button or Escape closes it and returns focus to the toggle; `boardOpen`, `defaultBoardOpen`, and `onBoardOpenChange` control it, and `boardToggleLabel` and `boardCloseLabel` rename the buttons. `RailTile` and `RailTileProps` draw a 44px navigation card tile in any `CardTone`, rendered as a link or any component passed through `as`; `label` is its accessible name and tooltip, `current` sets `aria-current="page"` and presses the tile flat, and `count` adds a badge whose value joins the accessible name through `countLabel`.

Icon supplies 62 single-ink glyphs: the 32 original design-system icons and 30 application additions. Its props are a typed `name`, optional numeric or CSS-length `size`, optional accessible `label`, and native SVG attributes/ref. It inherits current text color and is decorative and unfocusable by default. Named images use `role="img"`; actions use the enclosing control's accessible name, keyboard handling, and focus. The full `Icon` and `iconNames` catalogue support runtime selection. `createIcon` plus individual definitions (for example `searchIcon`) builds a typed subset so unused glyphs are tree-shaken; `IconName`, `IconProps`, `IconDefinition`, and `NamedIconProps` describe these exports.

`Badge` and `Sticker` accept `size` through `BadgeProps` and `StickerProps`, and `tone` in any `ExtendedTone`. The shared `MarkSize` covers `default`, `sm`, and `count`. Count marks fit one or two digits in an 18px height. Round stickers measure 64px by default, 36px at `sm`, and 18px at `count`, with `--kraft-sticker-size` available for a custom diameter. Both marks keep labels on one line and retain their width in flex rows.

`StatusDot` and `StatusDotProps` draw a small state mark in a required `StatusTone` (`working`, `waiting`, `blocked`, `done`, `idle`). It takes visible `children` or an accessible `label`, so colour is never the only signal; the tones also differ in form by default. `StatusDotVariant` (`solid`, `hollow`, `dashed`) and `StatusDotShape` (`round`, `square`) override the form, and `StatusDotSize` covers 8, 10, and 12px. A cut ring in the theme's backing keeps every dot at 3:1 on each material in both themes. `StatusSticker` and `StatusStickerProps` print the state in small capitals with `StatusStickerVariant` (`solid`, `paper`, `ghost`), an optional decorative `StatusStickerIndicator` (`dot`, `spinner`), and `StatusStickerSize` (`sm`, `md`); its lengths follow the font size from 8 to 12px. Both are noninteractive, stay upright, and pass native props and the ref to the outer span. `AvatarStatus` uses the same `StatusTone`.

`Banner` and `BannerProps` draw a full-width paper strip with a `control` slot at `controlSide` (`start` or `end`), an optional bold `title`, a line of consequence in `children`, and a shared `tone`. `Callout` and `CalloutProps` draw a bordered `role="note"` with `title`, decorative `leading`, `action`, and `tone`; the danger tone with an action is the danger form. Both are their own paper and keep every ink role at 4.5:1 on the tint. `EmptyState` and `EmptyStateProps` draw a dashed placeholder with `title`, `titleAs` (a paragraph by default), a note in `children`, decorative `leading`, `action`, and `size` (`sm`, `md`), in the ink of the surrounding material. `Toast` and `ToastProps` draw a kraft slip that is a polite atomic status with `leading`, `onUndo` and `undoLabel`, `onDismiss` and `dismissLabel`, and an optional `duration` that restarts after hover or focus leaves. `ToastDismissReason` is `close`, `timeout`, or `undo`; Escape dismisses a focused toast and focus returns to the element that had it when the toast appeared. `ToastRegion` and `ToastRegionProps` provide the named landmark that stacks toasts at a `ToastPlacement` of `bottom`, `bottom-end`, `top`, or `top-end`. All forward native props and refs to a div.

`Stat` and `StatProps` print a handwritten `value` over a `label` with a shared `tone`, a `StatSize` of `sm`, `md`, or `lg`, and `align`. `Timeline` and `TimelineProps` render `items` as a native ordered list in a `TimelineOrientation` of `vertical` (ruled history rows) or `horizontal` (a schedule lane that becomes a scroll region named by `scrollLabel` when it overflows). A `TimelineItem` has `id`, `title`, and optional `time`, `dateTime`, `description`, `trailing`, a `StatusTone` marker `tone`, and `current`, which sets `aria-current="time"`. `Stepper` and `StepperProps` render `steps` as a static ordered list in a `StepperVariant` of `pipeline` or `track`. A `StepperStep` has `id`, `label`, and optional `description`, `trailing`, and a `StepStatus` of `done`, `current`, `upcoming`, or `blocked`. Marks differ in form as well as colour, each label is followed by its state in words that `statusLabels` can rename, and the current step has `aria-current="step"`.

`Meter` and `MeterProps` draw a ruler that is one `role="meter"`: a single `value` with a `tone`, or `segments` of `MeterSegment` (`id`, `label`, `value`, `tone`, and a `MeterFill` of `solid`, `hatched`, or `free`), with `min`, `max`, `ticks`, `tickLabels`, a `limit` mark with a spill past it, a `legend`, `formatValue`, `valueText`, and a `MeterVariant` of `ruler` or the compact `bar`. `SlotMeter` and `SlotMeterProps` draw one paper slot per place under `cap`; `slots` takes a `StatusTone` or `SlotMeterSlot` for each taken place, with `SlotMeterSize` (`sm`, `md`), `toneLabels`, and `valueText`. `Gauge` and `GaugeProps` draw a half dial with a needle for `value` between `min` and `max`, with `tone`, `limit`, `GaugeSize` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`), `showValue`, `formatValue`, and `valueText`. All three expose their value, range, and a text alternative, take their name from `label` or `aria-labelledby` (`MeterName`), differ in form as well as colour, and keep 3:1 on every material in both themes. `Sparkline` and `SparklineProps` draw a bar for each `SparklinePoint` in `data` as one named image whose text alternative reads every point or a `summary`, with `max`, `limit`, and `SparklineSize` (`sm`, `md`). None is interactive; native props and refs reach the outer element. The gauge needle follows the motion setting and system reduced motion, and its figure follows theme tilt.

`TagInput` and `TagInputProps` provide a field of removable Tag slips with a native add input for use inside FormField. They take `value` or `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `name`, `variant`, `removeLabel`, `tagsLabel`, `inputClassName`, and `inputStyle`; native input props and the ref reach the add field. Enter or a comma adds a trimmed, unique slip, Backspace in the empty field moves to the last remove button, and focus follows a removal. `Pager` and `PagerProps` provide a navigation landmark with previous and next buttons, an optional polite `range`, and optional page `dots`; they take `pageCount`, `page` or `defaultPage`, `onPageChange`, `label`, `previousLabel`, `nextLabel`, and `pageLabel`. `Breadcrumb` and `BreadcrumbProps` render `BreadcrumbItem` entries (`label`, optional `href`, `id`, and `current`) as an ordered list in a named landmark, mark the current page, and accept `linkAs`. `Toolbar` and `ToolbarProps` provide a labelled `role="toolbar"` with one roving tab stop moved by the arrow keys, Home, and End; `ToolbarGroup` and `ToolbarGroupProps` cluster controls with an optional `label` and `align` of `start` or `end`. `CollapsibleGroup` and `CollapsibleGroupProps` provide a native `details` disclosure with `title`, `count`, `countLabel`, a `CardTone` header tint, `open` or `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, and a shared `name` for one-open-at-a-time groups.

`Stamp` and `StampProps` print text `children` as an outlined ink impression with an optional `signature` cell. `tone` supports neutral, blue, sage, danger, and attention, and `StampSize` supports `sm`, `md`, and `lg`. Use the default `as="span"` for a static stamp or `as="button"` for a controlled toggle with `pressed` and native `onClick`. The button exposes its state with `aria-pressed`, a dashed or solid impression, and visible keyboard focus. Its text supplies the accessible name. Native props and refs reach the rendered element. The ink follows theme tilt, and opt-in `animate` follows theme motion and system reduced-motion preferences.

`Tag` and `TagProps` display text `children` with an optional decorative `leading` icon or CSS color `swatch`. `TagVariant` supports `kraft` (default) for punched luggage tags with monospaced identifiers and `paper` for workspace labels. Both truncate long text while keeping the full label available to assistive technology. Use a static `span`, `as="a"` with `href`, or `as="button"` with a default `type="button"`. The ref and native props reach the chosen element, with `className` and `style` on the outer tag. `onRemove` and `removeLabel` add a separately named close button with its own visible keyboard focus. The application manages removal and focus afterward. Optional numeric `tilt` follows the theme, and both forms respect Paper and Night textures, motion settings, and system reduced motion.

`Checkbox` and `CheckboxProps` retain native checkbox props, form behavior, and input refs. The row has optional decorative `leading` media, `description` and `trailing` text announced as descriptions, and a full-row label hit target aligned to the first line. `strikeWhenChecked` strikes the label in the checked state. `indeterminate` shows a mixed state. `CheckboxVariant` offers `"ink"` and `"pen"`. `CheckboxStatus` and `CheckboxStatusProps` expose a large static span with a required accessible `label`, `checked`, `indeterminate`, and `variant`. `CheckboxGroup` and `CheckboxGroupProps` expose a native fieldset and ref with a `label` legend, vertical or horizontal `orientation`, optional maximum `columns`, dashed row separators, and an optional application-supplied `tally`. Each child keeps native form and keyboard behavior.

`Toggle` and `ToggleProps` retain native checkbox props, form behavior, and input refs under `role="switch"`. The switch is drawn as a card pull-tab in a slot that shows a handwritten “on” or “off” and turns blue when on. The written state is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. `children` name the switch and `description` is announced as its description. `labelSide` (`ToggleLabelSide`: `end`, the default, or `start`) places the label after or before the switch. `start` stretches the row so the name starts it and the switch ends it, and the whole row stays the label. The slide follows the motion setting and system reduced motion, and reverses in right-to-left text.

`FormField` and `FormFieldProps` associate a visible label, description, and optional error with one native control. `labelStyle="uppercase"` prints a small uppercase label. `TextField` and `TextFieldProps` offer `variant="boxed" | "underline"` and `size="sm" | "md" | "lg" | "title"`, with a handwritten title size and support for native numeric input sizes. `Textarea` and `TextareaProps` offer `variant="boxed" | "ruled"`, `mono`, `autoGrow` from the native `rows` height, and a `footer` slot for counters or supporting text. `SearchField` and `SearchFieldProps` provide a native search input with `icon` to replace or remove the leading mark, `trailing` for a key hint or count that stays clear of the native clear button, and `inputClassName` and `inputStyle` for the input; its `className` and `style` size the whole field. All three controls forward native props and refs. `Kbd`, `KbdProps`, and `KbdSize` draw a keycap for one key or, through `keys`, a chord of nested `<kbd>` elements, in `sm` or `md` sizes.

`Composer`, `ComposerProps`, and `ComposerSendKey` provide the prompt box: a red cardboard frame around a ruled paper card that wraps one native textarea with a handwritten placeholder. The paper grows from `rows` to `maxRows` lines and then scrolls. `sendKey` is `enter` (the default: Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line) or `mod-enter` (Ctrl+Enter or ⌘+Enter sends). The keys are shown as `Kbd` hints that describe the textarea, and `hints` replaces or removes them. The send key calls `onSend` with the text and submits an enclosing or associated form through its submit button, as a single-line input does. Blank text, a held key, and input-method composition do not send. `bar` holds application controls, and `mic` and `send` hold actions drawn as discs; the application owns them. `busy` holds the send key, marks the textarea busy, and shows a Pinwheel with `busyLabel` in a polite status. `disabled` disables the textarea and quiets the frame. Native textarea props and the ref reach the textarea, `className` and `style` size the frame, `inputClassName` and `inputStyle` reach the textarea, and a `FormField` supplies its label, description, and error. The frame rises while focus is inside it, following the motion setting and system reduced motion, and it never tilts.

`InlineEdit` and `InlineEditProps` show a handwritten title that becomes a named text input on click or focus. They take `label`, `value` or `defaultValue`, `onValueChange`, `placeholder`, `titleAs`, and `disabled`. Enter commits and Escape cancels, then focus returns to the title button. Blur commits while focus continues to its destination. Native div props and the ref reach the wrapper.

`Select` and `SelectProps` provide an accessible, single-choice listbox with form submission and a native button ref. `variant="compact"` fits the chosen option’s label, `leading` icon, and decorative `statusDot` into a shorter trigger. `SelectOption.statusDot` uses a shared `Tone`. The option label conveys status in text and the field keeps its accessible name when `hideLabel` is set.

`RadioGroup` and `RadioGroupProps` take `label`, `name`, `options`, `value` or `defaultValue`, `onValueChange(value, option)`, `description`, `orientation`, `hideLabel`, `required`, and `disabled`. `variant` (`RadioGroupVariant`) is `rows` (paper rows, the default), `flat` (only the picked row carries paper), `swatch` (a row of colour swatches), or `picture` (picture-only options). `columns` lays options out as equal-width cards and overrides `orientation`; horizontal rows share the width equally, so descriptions do not push them onto separate lines. Each `RadioOption` has a unique `value`, a `label`, and optional `description`, `leading`, `trailing`, `swatch` (any CSS colour), `picture`, and `disabled`. The trailing slot is not width-capped and can hold a handwritten note or a `Stamp`; when an option is too narrow to hold it beside the label and description, it moves to its own line below them. Swatch and picture radios are named by `aria-label`, with no visible or CSS-clipped label text, and their descriptions are referenced from hidden elements; they have targets of at least 44px, a ring around the picked swatch or picture, and a separate focus outline. Native fieldset props and the ref reach the fieldset, and `form` associates every radio with an external form.

`SegmentedControl` and `ToggleChips` expose `SegmentedControlProps`, `ToggleChipsProps`, and the shared `SegmentOption`. Each group takes `label`, `name`, `options`, `hideLabel`, `disabled`, `variant="joined" | "chips"`, and `tone="highlighter" | "ink"`. Each option has a unique `value` and a visible `label`, with optional `count`, `description`, and `disabled`. Counts and second lines describe the native inputs. Both accept `value`, `defaultValue`, and `onValueChange`. `SegmentedControl` uses one string and supports `required`. `ToggleChips` uses an array of strings. Named radios provide arrow-key movement. Checkboxes support Tab and Space. Native fieldset props and refs pass through, including external `form` association. Both submit enabled checked inputs under their form name. Selection uses a dot or checkmark alongside the fill and keyboard focus has its own outline.

`ActionableList`, `ActionableListProps`, `ActionableListItem`, and `ActionableListItemProps` provide named flat collections with native list and item refs. A row has a link with `href`, a button with `onActivate`, or static content. `TreeView`, `TreeViewProps`, and `TreeNode` provide a named hierarchy with a native list ref, arrow-key navigation, selection, expansion, and typeahead. `textValue` overrides the rendered label for typeahead. `TreeBranchStyle` offers `"board"` and `"plain"` through `branchStyle`.

`RowDensity` offers `"default"` and `"compact"` through `density` on lists, list items, and trees. Single-line compact rows start at 28px and grow to fit their content. Shared `RowContentOptions` provide rich `label` and `description`, `truncateLabel`, `clampDescription`, `done`, and separate `actions`. Noninteractive `trailing` content sizes to fit the available row width. Done rows strike their titles and use muted ink. `RowActionsVisibility` offers `"always"` and `"hover"` through `actionsVisibility`. Hover actions reserve space, appear on hover or focus, and stay visible on touch. Native semantics, server rendering, visible focus, and readable ink apply in Paper and Night with decoration on or off.

`Tabs` and its exported `TabsProps` and `TabItem` support `panel="paper"`, `panel="bare"` for a Drawer or Dialog, and `panel="external"` for application-rendered content linked with `tabId` and `panelId`. Built-in panes take `content` and use `fill` to scroll independently within the available height. Tabs accept a `description` below the label and noninteractive `trailing` content for counts and status marks. `iconOnly` with a `leading` icon preserves the accessible label and reveals it in a tooltip. Overflow cues show where more tabs are available in either orientation.

`Table` and `TableProps` expose `columns`, `rows` or `groups`, `caption`, `empty`, `maxHeight`, and `scrollLabel`. A `TableColumn` supplies `id`, `header`, `align`, and `rowHeader`. `TableRow` supplies `id`, `cells`, `current`, and `dimmed`. `TableGroup` supplies `id`, `caption`, and `rows`. The table uses native headers, an ink rule under the sticky column heads, separate row groups, dashed row rules, and a highlighter mark for the current row. `TableRowAction` and `TableRowActionProps` provide a native button for opening details with pointer or keyboard. The application owns the current row and the drawer.

`DescriptionList` and `DescriptionListProps` display native label-value pairs using `items`, `columns={1 | 2}`, and `layout="stacked" | "inline"`. Each `DescriptionListItem` supplies `id`, `label`, `value`, and optional `action` and `wide`. The two-column layout fits narrow drawers by flowing to one column as space runs out.

`Prose` and `ProseProps` set reading rhythm for headings, paragraphs, lists, links, rules, tables, inline code, code blocks, quotes, and todo items placed inside it, using `as="div" | "article" | "section"`. A list item that starts with a checkbox strikes through when ticked. `Code` and `CodeProps` draw inline code as a chip on a faint strip. `CodeBlock` and `CodeBlockProps` show a recessed monospaced block from `code` with optional `language`, `copyable`, `copyLabel`, `lineNumbers`, `added`, `removed`, and `scrollLabel`. Added and removed lines use native `ins` and `del` with a sign as well as a tint. `Blockquote` and `BlockquoteProps` show a native quotation beside a thick highlighter rule.

`FormSection` and `FormSectionProps` group form content in a paper `<section>` named by its serif `title`. The optional `hint` appears in handwriting beside the title, and `children` hold the controls below. Set `titleAs` (`h2` by default) to match nested heading levels. `PageHeader` and `PageHeaderProps` render a sticky native `<header>` with `title`, `titleAs` (`h1` by default), and optional `back`, `leading`, `status`, and `actions` slots above a dashed rule. Both forward native root props and refs. Place the header above an independently scrolling edit form to preserve visible keyboard focus. Compose save feedback with a persistent live status for “All saved”, “Unsaved changes”, and a brief “Saved” confirmation. Compose setting rows with a visible name and note, optional metadata, and a native switch labelled and described by the row text.

`Dialog`, `DialogProps`, and `DialogSize` provide a native modal with controlled `open` and `onOpenChange`. Pass rich `title` and `description` content, an optional `titleAs`, and use `leading`, `eyebrow`, `actions`, and `footer` for header and footer content. Choose `size="sm" | "md" | "lg"`. The top edge stays anchored as content grows or shrinks. The browser confines focus and returns it when the modal closes. Set `initialFocusRef`, `dismissible`, or `closeLabel` to customize focus and dismissal.

`Drawer` and `DrawerProps` provide a native aside with rich `title` and `description` and slots for `leading`, `eyebrow`, `actions`, and `footer`. Supply `onClose` to show the close button and use `closeLabel` to name it. Set `footerPlacement="scroll"` to scroll it with the body.

`ResizablePane` and `ResizablePaneProps` fill a bounded container with scrolling flex columns for `main` and `pane`. The side pane supports stacked drawers and takes the full width in narrow containers. The application controls `open`, `side`, and focus return. Set `defaultSize` or control `size` and `onSizeChange`, with optional `minSize` and `maxSize`. The divider takes `resizeLabel` and supports pointer dragging, arrows, Home, and End. Use `mainLabel` and `paneLabel` to name overflowing columns for keyboard navigation.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Kraft UI. Preserve its established paper, cardboard, and sticker identity. Its character is tactile, calm, and playful.
