# @randalvance/kraft-ui

## 0.7.0

### Minor Changes

- 36c74c6: `CorkBoardColumn` now renders its name as an `h2` by default, where it was an `h3`. A board directly under the page `h1` no longer skips a heading level, which axe reports as `heading-order`. Pass `headingLevel={3}` for a board that sits under an `h2`, which is also how to keep the previous output. The README and stories now say how to choose the level for a column name and for a heading placed in a `StickyNote` header.
- 592cfa9: `Card` and `CardAddTile` gain `size="compact"`, which drops the `10rem` base height so the card or tile is as tall as its content. Use it for cards that hold a line or two, such as cards on a board, in a list, or in a thread. The default is unchanged, and `--kraft-card-min-height` still sets the base height of default cards. Adds the `CardSize` type.
- b48181c: Add `Composer`, the prompt box: a red cardboard frame around a ruled paper card that wraps a native textarea and grows with the text from `rows` to `maxRows` lines. Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a line, or `sendKey="mod-enter"` sends on Ctrl+Enter or ⌘+Enter. The keys are shown as `Kbd` hints that describe the textarea, and `hints` replaces or removes them. The send key calls `onSend` and submits an enclosing form through its submit button, as a single-line input does. `bar` holds the application's controls, and `mic` and `send` hold actions drawn as discs. `busy` holds the send key and shows a Pinwheel with `busyLabel`, and `disabled` quiets the frame. Native textarea props and the ref reach the textarea, and a `FormField` supplies its label, description, and error. Adds the `ComposerProps` and `ComposerSendKey` types.
- 1f4908a: `Button`, `IconButton`, `Badge`, and `Sticker` take six more tones: `ochre`, `teal`, `violet`, `plum`, `blush`, and `navy`. Use them to give a workspace, a rail tile, or a model chip its own colour. The new `ExtendedTone` type names the five shared tones and these six, and `CardTone` is now the same set under its old name. `Tone` is unchanged.
  
  Each new tone has three tokens with Paper and Night values: a face, a deeper cut edge, and an on-colour ink, named like `--kraft-teal-face`, `--kraft-teal-edge`, and `--kraft-on-teal`. A label keeps 4.5:1 on its face in both themes, with texture on or off. In Paper the teal, violet, and plum faces are deeper than `--kraft-teal`, `--kraft-violet`, and `--kraft-plum`, which cannot hold a label in either ink.
- 48102a9: Add twelve small components for feedback and navigation. `Banner` is a full-width paper strip that pairs a `control` with a line of consequence, and `Callout` is a bordered note with an optional `action`, including a danger form. `EmptyState` is a dashed placeholder with a note and an optional action. `Toast` is a kraft slip with Undo, a close button, and an optional `duration` that waits while hovered or focused; `ToastRegion` is the landmark that stacks toasts. `Stat` prints a large handwritten figure over a caption in five tones. `Timeline` draws ruled history rows or a horizontal schedule lane, and `Stepper` draws a vertical pipeline or a compact step track with `done`, `current`, `upcoming`, and `blocked` steps. `TagInput` is a field of removable slips with an inline add field for use in `FormField`. `Pager` has previous, next, a range, and page dots; `Breadcrumb` marks the current page; `Toolbar` and `ToolbarGroup` give grouped actions one roving tab stop with start and end groups; and `CollapsibleGroup` is a native disclosure with a tinted header, a count, and one-open-at-a-time groups through a shared `name`. Adds the `BannerProps`, `CalloutProps`, `EmptyStateProps`, `ToastProps`, `ToastDismissReason`, `ToastRegionProps`, `ToastPlacement`, `StatProps`, `StatSize`, `TimelineProps`, `TimelineItem`, `TimelineOrientation`, `StepperProps`, `StepperStep`, `StepperVariant`, `StepStatus`, `TagInputProps`, `PagerProps`, `BreadcrumbProps`, `BreadcrumbItem`, `ToolbarProps`, `ToolbarGroupProps`, and `CollapsibleGroupProps` types, and the `--kraft-content-sage` ink role for green text that stays readable on paper, kraft, and cardboard.
- 9bdcae9: `Mat` gains `tone="red"`, the red cardboard of the `Composer` frame. The face is `--kraft-mat-red` and the cut edge is `--kraft-mat-red-2`, in both Paper and Night.
- d372e54: Add `Meter`, `SlotMeter`, `Gauge`, and `Sparkline` for showing how much of something is used. `Meter` draws a ruler from one `value` or from `segments` with `solid`, `hatched`, or `free` fills, with `ticks`, `tickLabels`, a `limit` mark, a spill past the limit, a legend, and a compact `variant="bar"`. `SlotMeter` draws one paper slot for each place under a `cap`, each in a status tone or empty. `Gauge` draws a half dial with a needle for a percentage, with an optional `limit` and three sizes. All three are one `role="meter"` with a value, a range, and a text alternative, named by `label` or `aria-labelledby`. `Sparkline` draws a small bar for each day of usage as one named image, with an optional `limit`. Adds the `MeterProps`, `MeterSegment`, `MeterFill`, `MeterVariant`, `MeterName`, `SlotMeterProps`, `SlotMeterSlot`, `SlotMeterSize`, `GaugeProps`, `GaugeSize`, `SparklineProps`, `SparklinePoint`, and `SparklineSize` types.
- b539f65: Add fourteen glyphs to `Icon`, so an application rail and its toolbars no longer need a local `createIcon` set. `bulb`, `clipboard`, and `board` are the design system's own ideas, artifacts, and tasks drawings. `moon` is the night mode toggle and `chevron-right` pairs with `chevron-left`. `sparkle`, `filter`, `graph`, `expand`, `sliders`, `activity`, `ban`, and `question` cover common toolbar actions. `editor` is a window with code in it, for opening something in an editor without a product's logo. Each has a named definition for `createIcon`, such as `moonIcon` and `chevronRightIcon`, and `iconNames` now lists 62 names.
- a726401: RadioGroup gains `variant` (`rows`, `flat`, `swatch`, `picture`) and `columns`. `columns` lays options out as equal-width cards, and horizontal rows now share the width equally even with descriptions. `flat` highlights only the picked row. `swatch` draws a row of ringed colour swatches from each option's `swatch` CSS colour, and `picture` shows each option's `picture`, for example a portrait; both name the radio with `aria-label` instead of a visible or clipped label. The trailing slot is no longer capped at 40% width, so it can hold a handwritten note. The picked row's description now prints in ink so it stays readable on the Night highlighter. `form` now reaches every radio. Adds the `RadioGroupVariant` type and the `swatch` and `picture` option fields.
- 293d9f0: Add `StatusDot` and `StatusSticker` for showing what state something is in. `StatusDot` draws a working, waiting, blocked, done, or idle mark in solid, hollow, or dashed form, round or square, at 8, 10, or 12px. It takes visible `children` or an accessible `label`, so colour is never the only signal, and each tone has its own default form. `StatusSticker` prints the state in small capitals as a `solid`, plain `paper`, or dashed `ghost` sticker, with an optional `dot` or `spinner` indicator, in `sm` and `md` sizes. Adds the `StatusTone`, `StatusDotProps`, `StatusDotVariant`, `StatusDotShape`, `StatusDotSize`, `StatusStickerProps`, `StatusStickerVariant`, `StatusStickerIndicator`, and `StatusStickerSize` types. `AvatarStatus` now uses `StatusTone`.
- 6cba36d: `Toggle` is now drawn as a pull-tab: a thick card tab slides in a slot cut into the paper, and the slot shows a handwritten “on” or “off” and turns blue when on. It is still a native checkbox with `role="switch"`; the written state is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. The switch is 56×26px instead of 44×26px. `Toggle` gains `labelSide` (`"end"` by default, or `"start"`): `"start"` puts the label before the switch and stretches the row, so a settings row reads name first and switch last while the whole row stays clickable. Hovering anywhere on the label now darkens the unchecked edge. The slide follows the theme motion setting and system reduced motion, and forced colours keep the written state legible. Adds the `ToggleLabelSide` type and the `--kraft-toggle-on-ink` custom property. The `.kraft-toggle-knob` class is replaced by `.kraft-toggle-slot`, `.kraft-toggle-word`, and `.kraft-toggle-tab`.

### Patch Changes

- 7b1fe57: `Card` with `as="a"` now accepts any node in its `media` slot. The anchor's native `media` attribute is a string, and its type was merged into the slot's, so `<Card as="a" href="…" media={<Avatar … />}>` failed to typecheck. Card's slot names now take precedence over native attributes of the same name on every variant. Nothing changes at runtime: `media` was always rendered as slot content and never reached the link as an attribute.
- 1c6a047: RadioGroup no longer squeezes an option's `trailing` content. The slot shrank together with the label and description and could break in the middle of a word, so a `Stamp` in a `columns` card printed a few letters per line, and short notes such as "Medium" broke in horizontal rows. The trailing content now keeps its natural width and the description wraps first. When an option cannot hold the trailing content beside 9rem of mark, label, and description, the trailing content moves to its own line below them, at the end edge. The mark, leading content, and copy now sit in a `.kraft-radio-main` wrapper inside the option.

## 0.6.0

### Minor Changes

- 7ff2ac7: Add AppShell for the diorama page layout: a rail of navigation tiles, an optional top bar, an optional cardboard board, and one paper sheet filling the viewport with a 3px margin, where each region scrolls on its own and the sheet can keep a fixed head. On narrow shells the board becomes a dismissible overlay opened from the rail. Add RailTile, a 44px navigation card tile in any card tone that renders any router link through `as`, presses flat for the current page, and carries a count badge and a tooltip.
- c6e5098: Add `Kbd` for keycap hints, one key or a chord such as `keys={['Ctrl', 'K']}`, in `sm` and `md` sizes. SearchField gains a `trailing` slot for a Kbd or a count that stays clear of the native clear button, and an `icon` prop to replace or remove the leading mark. SearchField's frame now draws the paper: `className` and `style` size the whole field, so it can share a flex row without a wrapper, and the new `inputClassName` and `inputStyle` reach the input. Adds the `KbdProps` and `KbdSize` types.
- b529585: Fix Night theme contrast. `--kraft-red-2` and `--kraft-blue-2` are now light tints in Night, so red and blue text is readable on night paper. A new `--kraft-blue-deep` token keeps the deep blue for button and checkbox edges, the Night toggle rail, the navy tone, and avatar marks. Use the documented `--kraft-content-danger` ink role for danger text: it follows the material and theme, and form errors and destructive menu items now use it. Destructive menu items in Night no longer use the raw `--kraft-red`. Night `--kraft-green` is lighter so a green dot stays visible on a highlighted (picked) row. Icons on a Badge or Sticker print in the mark's ink, so a yellow glyph on an attention Sticker stays visible. Checkbox and toggle faces no longer fade between colors, so switching themes doesn't flash unchecked checkboxes dark.
- 565b7ae: Widen the type range. Text gains `serif` and italic `lead` variants, a `size` for the hand variant from 17px to a 52px display size, and `faint`, `danger`, and `attention` tones that stay readable on paper, kraft, and cardboard in both themes. Heading gains handwritten `title-sm` and `display` sizes. `measure={false}` on Text and `balance={false}` on Heading turn off the 72ch cap and balanced wrapping, and code text in a `pre` is no longer capped. Link gains `tone="inherit"` and `underline="hover"` for links inside headings. Adds the `TextVariant`, `TextTone`, `HandSize`, `HeadingSize`, and `LinkProps` types and nine `text-*` ink tokens.
